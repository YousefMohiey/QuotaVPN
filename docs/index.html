How it works. Three steps, then one honest sentence.

  1. 01Pick a package. Gamerz or Streamerz, one decision per session.
  2. 02Connect. One circle runs the session on both apps.
  3. 03It bills there. The session counts against that package, not the general pool.

Underneath: your ISP sorts connections by reading the server name in the TLS handshake, so the app puts your package's server name into that handshake. Standard does this over VLESS on TCP 443; WireGuard and Hysteria2 are raw UDP with nothing to read, so they spend general quota.

What it does.

  • Gamerz / Streamerz
    Two packages, two server lists, one decision per session.
  • Per-app routing
    Whole device, only these apps, or everything except them, on both apps.
  • Valorant voice chat
    Region-blocked voice goes through the VPN while the match stays direct.
  • Speed test
    Ping, jitter, download and upload against your own server, with history.
  • Three transports
    Standard keeps the package quota; WireGuard and Hysteria2 spend general quota.
  • Self-updating
    Windows installs signed releases itself; Android updates from inside the app.
