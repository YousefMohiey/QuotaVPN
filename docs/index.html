Underneath

Your ISP sorts connections by reading the server name in the TLS handshake, so the app puts your package's server name into that handshake. Standard does this over VLESS on TCP 443; WireGuard and Hysteria2 are raw UDP with nothing to read, so they spend general quota.

What it does.

Every item ships on Windows and Android

  • Gamerz and Streamerz

    Whole device, only these apps, or everything except them, on both apps.

  • Valorant voice chat

    Region-blocked voice goes through the VPN while the match stays direct.

  • Speed test

    Ping, jitter, download and upload against your own server, with history.

  • Three transports

    Standard keeps the package quota; WireGuard and Hysteria2 spend general quota.

    Windows installs signed releases itself; Android updates from inside the app.
