# QuotaVPN 0.7.6-exp.12

Windows experimental build. The phone stays at 0.7.5.

## Themes

- Quota is the default palette. The old Serein name is gone.
- Seven new presets use their official palettes: Catppuccin, Tokyo Night, Nord,
  Dracula, Gruvbox, Rosé Pine and One Dark.
- Each one moves the surfaces, hairlines, text, semantic colours and accent
  together, not just the highlight.
- Appearance is flat now: a row of palette orbs, the primary colour picker and
  the interface font picker. The old preview cards and the rail swatches are
  gone.
- Old saved theme names migrate onto the closest new palette.

## Fonts

- Inter is the default interface face.
- Geist, Manrope, IBM Plex Sans, Archivo and JetBrains Mono are bundled. The
  installed Windows faces remain.
- The font picker previews every family in its own face and uses the right
  search placeholder.
- Geist Mono stays on addresses, latency and other data.

## Everything from exp.11

The shipping icon is unchanged. Only its royal-blue tail is recoloured to the
active accent, so the black tile, the white Q and the anti-aliased rim stay as
drawn. Primary button labels still follow the accent, with white on the deep
Quota blue and black on the pale presets.
