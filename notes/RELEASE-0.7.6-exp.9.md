# QuotaVPN 0.7.6-exp.9

Windows experimental build. The phone stays at 0.7.5.

## Spaces

- There is no automatic default space any more. The page starts empty and the
  user creates the first space.
- Nothing connects on its own. Select a space and press Connect. The old
  auto-switch on selection and on launch is gone.
- The button now says Connect and Disconnect, not "Turn on LAN".
- The page is shorter: the space name, the status, the peers, the invite and
  Remove. The play steps, the long hints and the per-space note are gone.
- The invite note and the dialogs are shorter.

The old private default network and the old space records were removed from
the app and the server. Create a new space and send its invite to the other PC.

## Everything from exp.8

Private spaces, sealed space passwords, manual switching, orphan-safe create
and delete, invite size cap, DPAPI card restore, parallel RTT checks, log
rotation, and the local YousefMohiey signature.
