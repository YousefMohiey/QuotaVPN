# QuotaVPN 0.7.6-exp.14

Windows experimental build. The phone stays at 0.7.5.

## Home

- Gamerz and Streamerz now live in one joined switch. The selected pill slides
  between the two with a softer spring and the icon gives a small pop.
- Choosing a preset shows a spinner in its card. The mouse no longer turns into
  the Windows wait cursor.
- The whole Server and App Routing cards are clickable, not just the chevron.
  The custom-domain input still owns its card while it is open.

## Speed

- The car-style needle and the nine crowded numbers are gone. The gauge is a
  slim track with one lit arc, a reading dot, five labels and the live number in
  the centre.
- The measuring strip is now a full-width bar under the gauge. The Stop control
  is a round red button at the far right with a pulsing ring, and a thin progress
  line crosses the bottom of the bar.
- The whole Selected Server card opens the server picker. The refresh icon stays
  a separate action.
- Refresh buttons no longer change the mouse cursor to a spinner; the icon
  itself shows the progress.

## Everything from exp.13

The interface scale, the space switching fix, the owner Leave flow and the
graceful space disconnect remain.
