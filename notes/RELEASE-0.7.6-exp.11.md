# QuotaVPN 0.7.6-exp.11

Windows experimental build. The phone stays at 0.7.5.

## Real icon

- The rail shows the shipping QuotaVPN icon again, not the drawn placeholder.
- Only the royal-blue tail is recoloured. The black tile, the white Q, the
  shading and the anti-aliased rim stay exactly as drawn.
- The tail follows all seven presets and a custom primary colour. A blended edge
  keeps its lower saturation, so the rim still fades into the tile.
- The Windows icon and the tray asset stay static.

## Everything from exp.10

Seven full colour presets, Appearance in Settings only, the primary colour
picker, the interface font picker, readable button labels, and the shorter
Spaces page from exp.9.
