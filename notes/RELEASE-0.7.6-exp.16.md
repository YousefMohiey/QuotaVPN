# QuotaVPN 0.7.6-exp.16

Windows experimental build. The phone stays at 0.7.5.

## Speed dial

- The Speed screen now follows the Speedtest by Ookla dial: a 270 degree arc,
  the nine original labels (0, 5, 10, 50, 100, 250, 500, 750, 1000), a
  translucent tapered needle and a small hub.
- The arc uses the theme accent in a soft gradient, and labels the needle has
  passed turn bright while the rest stay muted.
- The idle state is the thin gradient ring around GO, matching Speedtest. The
  ring uses the theme accent instead of a fixed bitmap.
- The live numbers stay in the reading rail, as they do above the Speedtest
  gauge. The full-width measuring bar, round Stop button and progress line stay.

## Preset switch

- Gamerz/Streamerz now uses a 0.5 second ease-out tween for the sliding pill,
  with a 0.35 second icon scale. It is slower and has no spring overshoot.

## Everything from exp.15

The Connection Type card fills its width and shows WireGuard, the whole-card
targets and normal refresh cursors remain, and the space fixes are unchanged.
