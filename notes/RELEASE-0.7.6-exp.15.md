# QuotaVPN 0.7.6-exp.15

Windows experimental build. The phone stays at 0.7.5.

## Home

- The Connection Type control now fills its card. Standard, Hysteria2 and
  WireGuard are all fully visible, with no empty space on the right.
- The Gamerz/Streamerz switch is slower and smoother: a softer spring for the
  sliding pill, a gentler icon pop and a longer colour transition.

## Speed

- The dial is a car cluster again. It has a dark face, a fine outer tick ring,
  a lit arc, a tapered needle and five labels: 0, 10, 100, 500 and 1000.
- The live number sits in a small digital capsule under the hub, with the phase
  and unit, instead of floating in the middle of the face.
- The idle state shows the faint cluster around the GO button, so the gauge is
  visible before a run.
- The full-width measuring bar, round Stop button and progress line from exp.14
  stay.
- The whole Selected Server card still opens the picker, and refresh controls
  keep the normal mouse cursor.

## Everything from exp.14

The whole-card Home targets, no wait cursors, the space switching fix, the
interface scale and the owner Leave flow remain.
